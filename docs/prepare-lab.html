Prepare the Lab Environment

Bring up the CML topology so it can support the automation scenarios that follow.

Remote access

Access the Ubuntu workstation GUI through the dCloud VM console. If the browser-based GUI is unavailable, connect with the Cisco Secure Client VPN and then SSH into the lab or reach the lab IPs directly in a browser.

Log in to CML

  1. Open the workstation

    On the dCloud topology page, open the ubuntu-25.04 workstation. Under Remote Access, choose VM Console to reach the desktop.

  2. Browse to CML

    From the workstation, open the Cisco Modeling Labs URL in any browser:

    CML URL
    http://198.18.1.2/

  3. Authenticate

    Enter username admin, password C1sco12345, and click Login. The CML dashboard appears.

Start the topology

  1. Start the lab

    Under the lab topology, click the Start button to power on the virtual devices.

  2. Wait for boot

    Allow approximately five minutes for the nodes to finish booting.

    Be patient

    IOSv and CSR1000v need time to fully initialize. Opening a console too early may show a blank screen — give the nodes time to reach the login prompt.

  3. Open the topology

    Click anywhere on the network diagram to enter the lab and begin accessing devices.

Open a device console

  1. Right-click a node

    Right-click a device to open the context menu, then choose Console for CLI access.

  2. Open the console

    Click Open Console. When prompted, log in with the router credentials.

    router login

    From each router, confirm the devices are up and note the interfaces you will be testing.
